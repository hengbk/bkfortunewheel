How to play

  • Spin the wheel (button, click the wheel, or press Space). Landing on a cash value lets you call a consonant: you earn that value for every time it appears.
  • Called a letter that's in the puzzle? You keep your turn. A miss passes play to the next player.
  • Buy a vowel for $250 from your round winnings.
  • Bankrupt wipes out your round winnings. Lose a Turn passes play.
  • Solve any time it's your turn. Get it right and your round winnings go to your bank (minimum $1,000). Wrong guesses pass play.
  • Highest bank after the final round wins. You can type letters on your keyboard too.
Install on your phone

iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome): tap Install app at the top, or open the ⋮ menu and choose Install app.
Once installed, it opens full screen and plays offline.
